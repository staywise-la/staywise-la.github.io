StayWise LA

Find a good-value stay in Los Angeles with one sentence. ISYE 6740 project (work in progress).

Module 1 will load vectors from web/data/ and search in the browser.

Module 2: fair price range and good-deal flags.

Module 3: neighbourhood market types and regions.

Module 4: complaint topics and per-listing pros and cons.

Data: Inside Airbnb, Los Angeles. Not affiliated with Airbnb.
